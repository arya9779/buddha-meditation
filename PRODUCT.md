# PRODUCT.md

register: brand
name: Buddha Meditation Center of Greater Washington DC
also_known_as: Mahamevnawa Meditation Center
vision: Meditation for anyone, regardless of background.

## Users

- First-time visitors who want stress relief and a quieter mind, not a religion.
- Regulars near Rockville who sit on Sundays and come to retreats.
- People anywhere joining the digital monastery on Zoom.
- Volunteers who once arrived as guests.
- A smaller group who want Theravada study under monks.

## Product purpose

The website is the months-long companion that holds a person from first landing to regular practice, then (if they want it) to service and study. Every page should leave the visitor with at least one useful next step, usually a sitting or the newsletter.

## Tone

Friendly, unhurried, specific. Speak like a person who already sat in the hall. Do not sell enlightenment. Do not sound like a corporation or a temple brochure that assumes the reader is Buddhist.

## Proof

- 6,000+ signups
- 4.90 / 5 program rating
- 82% reported relief from sadness after one sitting
- 75% reported less stress

## Anti-references

- The old WordPress navy (`#1C244B`) and amber (`#D68B4B`)
- Generic AI-generated meditation stock
- Identical icon-card grids
- Heavy enterprise layouts
- Inter-on-cream SaaS templates
- Cormorant / Playfair / Fraunces “luxury serif” headlines (the 2025–26 AI tasteslop fingerprint, including the sister site’s Cormorant + Inter pairing)

## Funnel (site structure follows this)

1. First visit feels like mindfulness for anyone.
2. Join a program (Meditate with a Monk, Sunday, Zoom).
3. Become a regular (retreats, weekly sits).
4. Connect on social.
5. Volunteer.
6. Optionally study: `/learn` and sutta class.

## Stack constraints

Astro, MDX for `/blog` and `/learn`, static hosting, GitHub org BMC-DC, maintainable by AI coding agents including Vidu.

The Metta Sutta is a training in goodwill that does not pick favorites. It is used in public sessions because people of every background can feel the instruction immediately.

The wish is simple:

May all beings be well.  
May all beings be happy.  
May all beings be free from suffering.

On Sunday evenings and at Meditate with a Monk, this often follows the breath. The two practices hold each other: the breath steadies the mind, loving-kindness warms it.

This teaching, the 16 steps of breathing meditation from the Ānāpānassati Sutta (MN 118), is one of the most complete practical instructions the Buddha gave.

It is not only a relaxation technique. Cultivated fully, mindfulness of breathing fulfills the Four Foundations of Mindfulness and the Seven Factors of Enlightenment, and leads to knowledge and freedom.

The practice begins simply:

- breathing in mindfully
- breathing out mindfully

Nothing is forced. The rest of the sixteen steps grow from that honesty with the breath.

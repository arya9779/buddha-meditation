The Kindness Crew at the center prepared meals for visiting monks, coordinated with public agencies, and helped hold a quiet field at the Lincoln Memorial.

Around 45,000 people gathered. No chanting as protest. No political banners. Presence.

A closing sitting at the Charles E. Smith Center at George Washington University held more than two hours of meditation in an arena. Students, neighbors, and guests sat together.

This is the public face of the work: a monastery that can also fill a civic space without turning it into a sermon.

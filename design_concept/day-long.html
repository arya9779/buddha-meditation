Day-long meditation retreat

A full Saturday of sitting, walking, and loving-kindness, with meals in noble quiet. For people who want more than an evening hour.

1st & 3rd Saturday, 9:00 am – 4:00 pm

Buddha Meditation Center, Rockville, MD

See upcoming datesPlan your visit

The day unfolds slowly: sitting, walking, a meal, more sitting. Monks guide the rhythm so you do not have to invent a schedule.

Monthly donors may attend day-long retreats as part of their pledge.

What is included

  • Sitting and walking meditation
  • Loving-kindness practice
  • Vegetarian meal
  • Guidance from resident monks

Who it is for

Beginners who can sit a full day, and experienced meditators.

Sit with this · one minute

Before you arrive, take one breath and let the shoulders drop on the way out. You have just meditated. The rest is the same thing, repeated with company.

Programs · Sunday evening meditation

Weekly, in person

Sunday evening meditation

Guided meditation followed by a Dhamma talk or Q&A. A steady weekly hour to come back to the breath, in the shrine room at the center.

Sundays, 5:00 – 7:00 pm

Buddha Meditation Center, Rockville, MD

Reserve a seatPlan your visit

Sunday evening is the center’s home practice. Arrive a little early, take a seat, and let the room settle around you.

The sitting is followed by a short talk or open questions with the resident monks.

What is included

  • Guided sitting
  • Walking practice as offered
  • Talk or Q&A
  • Community after the sit

Who it is for

Anyone seeking a regular in-person practice.

Sit with this · one minute

Before you arrive, take one breath and let the shoulders drop on the way out. You have just meditated. The rest is the same thing, repeated with company.
